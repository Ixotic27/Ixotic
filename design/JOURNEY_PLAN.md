# First-person illustrated house

Latest user direction supersedes HOUSE_PLAN.md.

- Fill the viewport with artwork. Start inside the study facing the iMac desk; retain the original SVG room's clear silhouettes. No coarse low-resolution rendering, blur, voxel people, outside captions, header, footer or room navigation.
- Perspective side walls, ceiling, floorboards and furniture faces convey depth from eye level. Native vertical scrolling advances study → library → kitchen → games room → exit door → closed house on a round earth. Zoom through the doorway between rooms; keep scrolling reversible.
- Study palette: blue slate #8e99a5, deep teal #354a4d, oak #c29c79, parchment #e1dfcb, sage #66827e, cat cream #f1dab2. Typography is readable Manrope with restrained monospace only inside devices and in-world signs.
- Only the daylight toggle is persistent outside the artwork. Scene objects are keyboard-accessible controls. Directions belong on in-world notes; music is controlled by an in-world speaker and M key.
- Cat moves and blinks. Replace the broken voxel character with illustrated figures or first-person coffee hands.
- Original procedural WebAudio motifs change per room and ending. Start only after user interaction, allow muting, pause when hidden, duck/pause for device dialogs. No paid service or external asset required.
- Exit door carries “Thanks for coming over.” The final pullback reveals a complete roofed house on a small round earth, with portfolio information moving along both sides. Reduced motion freezes incidental animation and moving text.
- Retain iMac/Game Boy content, independent library journals and table-tennis gameplay. Preserve old components as references.
